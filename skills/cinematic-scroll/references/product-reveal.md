# Product reveal recipe

Use for a launch, object story, feature explanation, mechanism, or comparison.

Before building, name one focal hierarchy and cause-and-effect, plus the type
contrast, density, product material, light, emphasis, and hardest midpoint. Keep
the product stable while one shared value changes its relevant state and labels.
Derive the visual language from the brief.

Choose one variable that explains the product: assembly, layer exposure, light
angle, material, calibration, scale, or a real before/after. Define what the visitor
learns at opening, during change, at the hold, and after release. Use CSS/SVG or
supplied stills for graphical states, local video for real temporal detail, and the
3D recipe when the brief requires geometry or camera travel.

1. Compose a stable product plate with headline, explanation, action, and a useful
   fallback image or authored graphic.
   Default to a relative track with a `100svh` sticky plate; stack the same states
   in normal flow on mobile and under reduced motion.
2. Create one progress function from real geometry. Drive product state, labels,
   light, and supporting atmosphere from this shared clock.
3. Allocate a clear establish interval, meaningful transformation, generous readable
   hold, and clean release. Keep direct scroll reversible.
4. Keep the clearest state as a permanent base. Transform child layers without
   fading the whole plate. Inspect 0/33/66/100%, hold, and reverse path.

With no installed motion library, prefer one scheduled scroll update and CSS
variables to multiple observers or experimental scroll timelines.

For video, provide a poster and dimensions. Confirm metadata loads, duration is
finite, seeking changes the visible frame, and decode failure reveals the poster.
A dense-keyframe local encode usually seeks better. Provider generation is optional
and outside this basic recipe.

Support the reveal only with evidence the brief supplies: detail, specification,
process, provenance, comparison, or use context. Keep the action available without
finishing the reveal. Mobile uses a shorter change or stacked before/after states;
reduced motion shows the clearest product state. Failed media preserves product,
copy, and action.

Accept only when the transformation communicates a real supplied fact, one clock
owns related state, labels settle without collision, reverse scroll works, and the
fallback states keep the same product and action.
