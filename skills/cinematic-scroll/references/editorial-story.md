# Editorial story recipe

Use for an image-led campaign, portfolio, essay, or branded longread.

Before building, name the focal asset, display/body contrast, material and light,
one or two motifs, and hardest transition frame. Keep the reading column stable
while imagery changes. Derive fonts, colors, density, imagery, and emphasis from
the brief rather than a reference brand.

1. Extract the supplied headline, body, captions, assets, action, and required
   order. Keep factual copy unchanged unless rewriting is requested.
2. Name a material/light world and two recurring motifs from the brand. Create only
   the beats the content earns: orientation, one signature reframe, evidence, and
   action are a useful starting shape rather than a quota.
3. Build the hero and signature beat first. Keep title and message in semantic HTML.
   Let the focal image, mask, crop, layered depth, or authored diagram carry motion
   while the reading column holds.
4. Derive one progress value from real section geometry. Map the signature state
   from it so reverse scroll retraces the change. Include a stable readable hold.
5. Inspect opening, midpoint, hold, exit, and reverse path before adding the rest.

Complete remaining beats with composition that suits their content: image scale,
alignment, or text measure may change while typography, spacing, emphasis, material,
and motifs retain coherence. Free-flow reading sections need no decorative pin.

On narrow screens, stack image and copy in the intended reading order and shorten
or remove pins. Reduced motion shows the best explanatory frame in normal flow.
Without JavaScript, all copy, images, navigation, and the primary action remain.

Accept only when the story order and supplied facts are complete; opening, midpoint,
hold, and close are composed; keyboard and browser-find order match the story; and
the result's hierarchy, typography, material, and action belong to the brief.
