# Page shapes

Choose the shape that answers the visitor's next question with available evidence.
Keep one primary action. The sequences below are starting structures, not quotas.
A review can establish clarity and completeness; improved conversion requires
separate measurement under stated conditions.

## Proof-first

**Use when:** one screenshot, working demonstration or concrete example makes the
product understandable without a long introduction.

**Section order:** outcome and action, the demonstration with its explanation,
supported benefits, objections answered beside relevant evidence, actual terms
or risk reversal, the same action.

**What proves it worked:** a review participant can describe the offer and next
step from the opening, locate the evidence for each benefit and find the action
without completing a decorative sequence. Record the observation. Do not turn
that clarity check into a conversion claim.

## Education-first

**Use when:** the visitor needs to understand a problem, mechanism or unfamiliar
category before the offer is credible.

**Section order:** recognisable situation, explanation of the missing understanding,
mechanism or process, supporting evidence, offer, remaining objections and terms,
primary action. Keep a route to the action available while the argument develops.

**What proves it worked:** the visitor can explain how the proposed approach relates
to their problem and identify what supports it. Check that each section answers
the question raised by the previous one and that the action remains reachable.

## Single-action

**Use when:** high-intent traffic already understands the subject and needs one
short offer, such as joining a waitlist or downloading a resource.

**Section order:** exact offer and intended recipient, essential conditions,
primary action, only the proof or reassurance needed to make that decision,
necessary privacy or delivery information.

**What proves it worked:** the visitor can name what they receive, what they must
provide and what happens next. No competing promotion interrupts the decision.
Verify the action's destination or form contract during implementation.

## Comparison

**Use when:** the visitor is choosing between alternatives and needs decision
criteria, tradeoffs and evidence rather than an unqualified superiority claim.

**Section order:** audience and decision, criteria, sourced comparison, fit and
limitations for each relevant option, offer and actual terms, primary action.

**What proves it worked:** the visitor can identify which option fits their stated
needs and trace material differences to current evidence. Mark unknowns explicitly.
Do not fabricate competitor facts, savings, prices or testimonials to fill a table.
