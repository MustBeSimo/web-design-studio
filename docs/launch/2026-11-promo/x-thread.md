1/6 A product story should look and move like the product it explains.

I built Web Design Studio: three free, open-source skills for coding agents, from the page's story to its motion and browser review.

Here's the 36-second film.

---

2/6 Plan with launch-page-strategy.

Choose what the page should say, prove and ask visitors to do.

Try: "Use launch-page-strategy to plan my waitlist page. Choose one action and write a story brief. Do not invent proof."

---

3/6 Build with cinematic-scroll.

Your brand, content and existing stack shape the result.

Try: "Use cinematic-scroll to build a product story with one scroll reveal. Keep my brand and copy. Verify mobile and reduced-motion states."

---

4/6 Review with scroll-audit.

Try: "Use scroll-audit to review my site's scroll experience. Score pacing, performance, accessibility and emotional arc. Write a remediation plan with the highest-impact fix first."
